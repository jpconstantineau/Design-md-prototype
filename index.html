Theme overview

Purpose

Evaluate how colors behave across semantic UI states.

This page demonstrates how a design system reacts when background, surface, border, text, accent, status, and interaction colors are adjusted for light and dark themes.

Type and content

Text contrast and hierarchy

Heading 2 visual rhythm

Lead text shows how the primary type color interacts with the page background and surface contrast.

Body text validates legibility against your chosen background and neutral surfaces, while captions and metadata confirm muted-text readability across the theme.

“Contrast is what makes a palette feel intentional. Test both default and semantic color roles to avoid unreadable combinations.”
  • List item styling reflects border and text color behavior.
  • Links should remain accessible in both light and dark themes.
  • Inline links should stand out without overwhelming bystanders.
