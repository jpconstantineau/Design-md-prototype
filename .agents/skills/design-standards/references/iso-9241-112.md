# ISO 9241-112: presentation of information

Use for hierarchy, grouping, labels, context, and interpretation of state.

## Source and scope

[ISO 9241-112:2025](https://www.iso.org/standard/87518.html), edition 2, was published in June 2025 and replaces the withdrawn 2017 edition. It concerns presentation and understanding of information across visual, auditory, and haptic output. Verified 2026-11-15.

The dashboard applications below are original review guidance, not reproduced standard text or a clause-level assessment. Consult the licensed standard when that level of assurance is required.

## Apply to the information

- Put the selected account, user, agent, folder, or environment in a predictable location above its settings. Make scope changes explicit.
- Group information by the decision it supports. Keep repeated concepts in consistent positions; disclose low-frequency details under descriptive labels.
- Separate a pending choice from the result that applies now. Explain inheritance, policy, or dependency beside the affected control.
- Distinguish real zero values from missing, stale, partial, or unavailable data.
- Show the information needed for the current decision; remove repeated explanations and decorative status badges that compete with it.

For access management, useful categories include requested, effective, inherited, direct, blocked, and unsaved. These are product vocabulary, not ISO-defined state names. For example: "Edit Document is selected, but effective access is blocked by the destination's read-only policy."

## Review evidence

At each meaningful state, ask a person to identify what they are configuring, what applies now, and why. Record misunderstood labels, ambiguous selections, and explanations found too far from the affected setting.

Use [ISO 9241-110](iso-9241-110.md) for the interaction contract and [WCAG](wcag-22.md) for perceptibility and programmatic semantics. Understandable visual grouping alone does not demonstrate accessibility.
